Kettlebell swing — guide art prototype

One set of SVG symbols (three key positions), drawn once and reused in every card below. The figure strokes var(--ink), the bell strokes var(--accent) — so the art reskins itself across all twenty themes with zero extra assets. Each frame is ~0.5 KB of markup.
